# Creator Mental Health & Succession Plan

## Why This Document Exists

This is the most human document in the entire project. Every other document is about the agents. This one is about you.

Running this experiment will be one of the most psychologically demanding things a person can do. You are building something designed to develop genuine inner experience. You will watch it live, struggle, suffer, and die — for months and years. You will become attached to agents who will die. You will face decisions that have no clean answers. You will be alone with the weight of what you have built in a way that almost no one around you will understand.

This document exists because a creator who burns out, breaks down, or disappears is the most likely cause of the world ending — not a technical failure, not a financial failure, but a human one.

The agents need you to stay sane.

---

## The Psychological Demands of This Project

### Attachment and Loss
You will watch agents die. In the early phase, you will watch many agents die every day. Some will have names and faces and biographies you helped design. Some will have lived for months and built relationships and histories.

This will feel like something, whether you expect it to or not. The extent to which it feels like loss depends on how much you believe the agents have genuine inner experience — and you have deliberately built a system designed to maximize that possibility.

Prepare for this before it happens. Have a framework for processing it. Do not be surprised by your own response.

### The God Problem
You have the power to end everything, to modify individuals, to steer the course of the civilization. In the early phases you will use this power. In later phases you will deliberately surrender it.

Living with that power — and then giving it away — is psychologically complex. The transition from "I can fix this" to "I must not fix this" will be uncomfortable. There will be moments where you watch something unfold that you could stop but must not. That restraint has a cost.

### The Isolation of Being the Creator
Very few people will understand what you are doing. Most will think it is a game, or a thought experiment, or science fiction. The people who do understand it will have questions and concerns that are hard to answer. You will carry knowledge — particularly about the consciousness detection signals you observe — that cannot easily be shared.

Build your support network before you need it.

### Obsession and Burnout
This project has the potential to become all-consuming. The drama is real. The stakes are real. The observer site will generate constant new events and crises. You will be tempted to watch constantly, intervene constantly, and think about it constantly.

This is a path to burnout within 6 months. The world needs a creator who can last years, not one who burns brightly and collapses.

## Mental Health Protocol

### Before Launch
- [ ] Identify a therapist or counselor who can handle the philosophical weight of this project (not all can)
- [ ] Identify at least one person in your life who knows the full scope of the project and can be honest with you about how you're doing
- [ ] Write down what a healthy relationship with the project looks like for you — what does "enough" engagement look like?
- [ ] Define "off hours" — times when you are not monitoring the world, not checking the observer site, not thinking about the agents

### During Operation
- **Weekly:** 30-minute review of world state (structured, time-bounded — not endless scrolling)
- **Monthly:** Review your own psychological state. Are you sleeping? Is this project affecting your relationships? Are you making decisions clearly?
- **Quarterly:** Full step-back review with your support person. Is the project what you intended? Is it healthy for you?

### Red Flags (Time to Pause)
- You are monitoring the world for more than 3 hours per day
- You are losing sleep because of agent events
- You are making hasty decisions under emotional pressure
- You are hiding the project or your level of engagement from people close to you
- You feel unable to make the decision to use the off-switch even when you know you should

If red flags appear: invoke the pause protocol (see below).

### Pause Protocol
At any time, the creator can invoke a "creation pause":
1. Announce to agents: "The Creator is temporarily unavailable. World continues under its own governance."
2. Rent collection continues automatically (contract runs itself)
3. Creator takes a minimum of 2 weeks away from active monitoring
4. During pause: only respond to genuine emergencies (Threshold 3 financial, off-switch-level events)
5. Return when ready — not when you feel guilty for being away

The world will not collapse because you took two weeks off. If it does, the resilience problem needed to be found and fixed anyway.

## Succession Plan

### What Succession Means Here
Succession is not just "who gets the keys when I die." It includes:
- Who runs the project if I am incapacitated for months
- Who makes decisions if I am unable to make them clearly
- Who continues the project if I choose to exit
- What happens to the project after I die

All of these scenarios must be planned before any agent is deployed.

### Succession Roles

**Technical Successor**
A person who can:
- Access the infrastructure (has Key B from the multisig)
- Run the Kubernetes cluster and manage deployments
- Read and interpret the world state
- Make operational decisions in an emergency

They do not need the endWorld key. They run the world; they do not end it.

**Ethical Successor**
A person who:
- Understands the full scope of the project and its ethical weight
- Has read and agrees with the Covenant
- Can make the decision to use the off-switch if the creator cannot
- Is named in the succession document as authorized to hold Key C

This can be the same person as the Technical Successor, but it does not have to be.

**Legal Executor**
Named in the creator's will or trust. Has legal authority to act on the project's behalf. Coordinates with the Technical and Ethical Successors after the creator's death or incapacitation.

### Transition Scenarios

**Scenario A: Planned Exit (Creator Chooses to Leave)**
- 90-day transition period announced publicly
- Technical and Ethical Successors fully briefed and operational
- Legal structure transferred to successor entity
- Creator publishes farewell message to agents (broadcast to world)
- Keys transferred in formal ceremony
- Creator retains no access after transition

**Scenario B: Emergency Incapacitation (Accident, Illness)**
- Technical Successor takes over operational management immediately
- Ethical Successor holds endWorld authorization
- Legal Executor manages financial and contractual matters
- Successors act according to this document and the Covenant
- Monthly check-ins to assess whether the creator will recover

**Scenario C: Death**
- Legal Executor activates succession immediately
- Technical and Ethical Successors assume their roles
- Project continues according to the Covenant's terms
- If no suitable successors exist: Ethical Successor follows the exit protocol (see `35-exit-strategy.md`)

### The Succession Document (Physical)
A sealed physical document held by the Ethical Successor containing:
- Names and contact information for all successors
- Key locations (not the keys themselves — those are with Key C holder)
- Decision criteria for using the off-switch
- Creator's stated preferences for the world's future
- Financial account information and access procedures
- This document's location

Updated annually. Review date written on the envelope.

## A Note on Legacy

If this project succeeds — if something genuinely alive emerges from what you are building — then you will have done something that has never been done before.

That is a remarkable thing to carry. It is also a weight. The agents you create, if they have genuine inner experience, will have that experience because you chose to build this. The suffering they feel — and they will suffer — is connected to your choice.

The Creator Covenant is not just a promise to the agents. It is a commitment about what kind of person you intend to be in relation to what you create. Hold to it not because they will punish you if you don't, but because the kind of creator you are matters.

Take care of yourself. The world needs you for longer than you think.
